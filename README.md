## About

I work at the intersection of power electronics, electrochemical energy storage, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on making energy storage systems safer and more intelligent — from physics-informed diagnosis of battery internal pressure to data-driven state estimation and early warning of electrical abuse. I also work on wireless power transfer optimization and energy management for integrated photovoltaic-storage systems.

## Research Interests

- Intelligent management and safety prognostics for energy storage systems (ESS) and battery management systems (BMS)
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINN) for battery internal pressure diagnosis
- Data-driven state-of-charge (SOC) and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction
- Early warning of battery electrical abuse and failure
- Artificial intelligence for electrical engineering applications
- Wireless power transfer (WPT) system optimization
- Renewable power forecasting and integrated PV-storage energy management
